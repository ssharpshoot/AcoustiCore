# Third-party data notice

AcoustiCore includes normalized product specifications and CEA/CTA measurement
records assembled from third-party comparison sheets and reviewers. Inclusion
does not transfer copyright or imply endorsement.

## Current publication boundary

- Measurement values are planning references, not AcoustiCore laboratory tests.
- Records without a specific source URL, licensing decision, and manual review
  remain `traceable_measurement` and can only produce a provisional result.
- CEA-2010-A, CTA-2010-B, and CTA-2010-C records remain separate.
- Product specifications and availability may be outdated.
- Rights holders may request correction, attribution, or removal through the
  project contact channel.

The file `data/primary-test-review.json` is the review queue. A record may be
promoted to `verified_measurement` only after its exact source, test conditions,
license status, and reviewer are recorded.
